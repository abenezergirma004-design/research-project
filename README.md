## Project Overview

This research examines digital information literacy and responsible generative AI use among Information Science students at Addis Ababa University. It focuses on how students use tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude when completing academic assignments and their ability to evaluate AI-generated academic information.

The study aims to assess students' information-evaluation skills, understand their AI-use practices, and identify challenges related to responsible AI use.

**Research Questions**

1. What is the level of digital information literacy related to evaluating AI-generated academic information among Information Science students at Addis Ababa University?
2. How do Information Science students at Addis Ababa University use generative AI tools when completing academic assignments?

## Research Objectives

**General Objective**

To assess digital information literacy and responsible generative AI use among Information Science students at Addis Ababa University.

**Specific Objectives**

- To assess students' ability to evaluate AI-generated academic information.
- To examine how students use generative AI tools when completing academic assignments.
- To identify challenges related to responsible generative AI use.

## Research Methodology

- **Research approach:** Quantitative
- **Research design:** Descriptive survey
- **Target population:** Information Science students at Addis Ababa University
- **Data collection instrument:** Anonymous questionnaire using Google Forms
- **Data analysis:** Descriptive statistics, including frequencies and percentages
